HOW TO RUN ON TWO MOBILES
STEP 01
Open on Laptop or Phone

Visit your live GitHub Pages URL on any screen or computer to view both QR codes.

STEP 02
Scan Phone 1 (Wheel)

Scan the left QR code with your first phone to load the Steering Wheel Controller.

STEP 03
Scan Phone 2 (HUD)

Scan the right QR code with your second phone & place it horizontally in front of you or near your windshield.

🚀 GITHUB PAGES 1-CLICK DEPLOYMENT GUIDE ▼
STEP 1
Commit & push these updated files (index.html, controller.html, hud.html) to your GitHub repository.
STEP 2
On GitHub, go to your repository and click Settings (top tab).
STEP 3
In the left sidebar, click Pages (under "Code and automation").
STEP 4
Under Build and deployment > Source, choose Deploy from a branch. Select main (or master) branch and folder / (root), then click Save.
STEP 5
Within ~30 seconds, GitHub gives you your live HTTPS link: https://<your-username>.github.io/<your-repo-name>/. Open it on your phone!
